# The Chessmaster 2000 (The Software Toolworks, 1986) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of The Chessmaster 2000 — the first of the Chessmaster
line, with the board, the analysis window and the move list.

## Rights

This one has a live successor, and that matters.

The Chessmaster series passed from The Software Toolworks through Mindscape to
Ubisoft, which published Chessmaster releases into the 2000s. The brand is not
abandoned.

The 1986 Macintosh build, however, is not part of any of that. No Chessmaster
product sold in the last thirty years contains it, and Ubisoft has not offered
it. The argument here is the same narrow one made for Lode Runner: the specific
artefact is out of commerce, even though the name is not.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `TheChessmaster2000_v102_4amMacCrack` — version 1.02 on a
Macintosh floppy, copy protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
