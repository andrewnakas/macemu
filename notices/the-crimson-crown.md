# The Crimson Crown (Penguin Software, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Crimson Crown, the sequel to Transylvania, published by Penguin Software
(later Polarware) — a graphic adventure with a picture window, a compass and
two companions you can send off on their own.

## Rights

Penguin Software became Polarware, which wound down in the early 1990s.
Polarware's founders have at times made some of the company's catalogue freely
available, but no such release covering this Macintosh build has been found,
and none is claimed here.

It has not been sold in decades and is in no current product.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `TheCrimsonCrownSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy with
its copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
