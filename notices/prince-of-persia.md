# Prince of Persia (Broderbund, 1992) — Macintosh

## What this is

The 1992 Macintosh port of Prince of Persia, in colour.

## Rights — a live rights holder

This needs stating carefully, because unlike most of the catalogue the owner is
active and large.

Jordan Mechner created Prince of Persia and Broderbund published it. Ubisoft
acquired the property in 2001 and has sold games under the name continuously
since: The Sands of Time and its sequels, the 2008 reboot, The Lost Crown in
2024, and a Sands of Time remake in development. Ubisoft is a live and
protective rights holder.

None of those products is this. They are new software sharing a name and a
premise. The 1992 Macintosh port has not been sold by Broderbund, Ubisoft or
anyone else in over thirty years, and is contained in no current product.

Mechner published the original Apple II source code himself on GitHub in 2012,
which says something about how the person who made it regards the 1989 game —
though it is the Apple II source, not this Macintosh port, and it grants nothing
in respect of this binary.

The argument here is abandonment of a specific artefact, not a claim on the
name. If Ubisoft disagrees, the takedown promise is unconditional.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `PrinceOfPersia_img` — a preserved 6 MB Macintosh disk.
The application and its data were copied onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk.
