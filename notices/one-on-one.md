# One on One: Dr. J vs. Larry Bird (Electronic Arts, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of One on One, Eric Hammond's basketball game for
Electronic Arts — Julius Erving against Larry Bird, half court, first to a
chosen score.

## Rights

Electronic Arts published it and still exists.

There is a second layer here worth naming: the game is built around two real
people, licensed by name and likeness in 1983. Those licences were for a
product that stopped being sold decades ago. Nothing about hosting this disk
should be read as a claim that anyone's likeness rights were renewed.

The 1985 Macintosh build is out of commerce. EA has not re-released it, and no
current product contains it.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `OneOnOneSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy with its copy
protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus. The
floppy carries two applications: the game, and a small utility named "fooo".
This copy names the game as its startup program.
