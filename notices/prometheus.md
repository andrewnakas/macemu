# Prometheus (Macintosh shareware, 1998)

## What this is

Prometheus 1.3 — a Macintosh arcade game. It runs in 256 colours or greys and
asks permission before changing the monitor to suit itself, promising to
restore the original setting when you quit.

## Licence

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `tucows_205693_Prometheus`, from the Tucows Macintosh
archive. The `.sit` expands to a plain folder, copied onto a title disk that
mounts beside the shared Mac OS 8.6 image.
