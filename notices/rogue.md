# Rogue (Epyx, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

The 1985 Macintosh release of Rogue, published by Epyx and developed by
A.I. Design (Michael Toy, Jon Lane and Glenn Wichman). It boots from a
preserved copy of its own floppy.

## Two different things called Rogue

The 1980 original — written by Michael Toy, Glenn Wichman and Ken Arnold at
Berkeley — was distributed with BSD and its source has been freely available
for decades. That version is not in question and is not what is hosted here.

This is the commercial port. A.I. Design took Rogue to the IBM PC and then the
Macintosh under contract to Epyx, and rebuilt the presentation around a
bitmapped screen and a mouse. The rules and the dungeon generator are Rogue's;
the artwork and the interface are new work made for money in 1985.

## Why it is hosted

Epyx filed for bankruptcy in 1993 and its catalogue was broken up and sold in
pieces. No rights holder has offered this version for sale in forty years, and
no current product contains it. The free BSD Rogue is widely available and is
a different artefact; someone looking for *this* one has, until now, had the
choice of an abandonware download or nothing.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `RogueSanIncCrack` — a 400K MFS floppy, copy-protection
removed by the preservationist known as san inc. The image is used as it is:
the disk names Rogue as the machine's startup program rather than the Finder,
so it boots directly into the game, which is what the original did.
