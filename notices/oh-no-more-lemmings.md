# Oh No! More Lemmings (Psygnosis, 1991) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh port of the Lemmings expansion: a hundred additional levels across
five new tilesets.

## Rights

The same lineage as Lemmings — DMA Design and Psygnosis, both now inside Sony.
Sony's Lemmings re-releases have not included this expansion, and the 1991
Macintosh port has not been sold by anyone in over thirty years.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `ohno_mac` — a preserved 7 MB Macintosh disk. The
application and its data were copied onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk.
