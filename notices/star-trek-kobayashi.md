# Star Trek: The Kobayashi Alternative (Simon & Schuster, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of Star Trek: The Kobayashi Alternative — a text
adventure with a real-time ship's clock, in which the Enterprise searches for
the missing USS Heinlein.

## Rights

Two separate layers, and one of them is very much alive.

**Star Trek** belongs to Paramount and is among the most actively exploited
properties in existence. Nothing here is a claim on it.

**The software** was published by Simon & Schuster's software division under
licence in 1985. That division is long gone, the licence is long expired, and
the program has not been sold by anyone in about forty years. No current Star
Trek product contains it.

A game built on a live licensed property is a weaker position than an orphan
work, and it is stated as such rather than glossed over. If Paramount or
Simon & Schuster object, it comes down at once.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `StarTrekTheKobayashiAlternativeSanIncCrack` — a
Macintosh floppy with its copy protection removed by the preservationist
working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
