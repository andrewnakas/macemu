# Temple of Apshai Trilogy (Epyx, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

The 1985 Macintosh Temple of Apshai Trilogy — Temple of Apshai, Upper Reaches
of Apshai and Curse of Ra, rebuilt for the Macintosh by Westwood Associates.

**The room-description booklet is not here.** The Dunjonquest games shipped
with a printed book of numbered prose descriptions and the program refers you
to it by number. That book was paper and is not on the disk.

## Rights

Epyx filed for bankruptcy in 1993 and its catalogue was dispersed rather than
retired. Parts of the "Games" series have surfaced in later compilations under
several different owners; who holds the Macintosh Apshai Trilogy today is
genuinely unclear, and no attempt is made here to pretend otherwise.

Nothing containing this build has been sold in decades.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `TempleOfApshaiTrilogy_1985_10_08_SanIncCrack` — the
1985-10-08 build on a Macintosh floppy, copy protection removed by the
preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
