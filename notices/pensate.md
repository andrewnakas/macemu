# Pensate (Penguin Software, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

Pensate, a chess-flavoured puzzle game by John Besnard, Macintosh version by
Robert Hardy, published by Penguin Software. You cross a board while pieces
that move in fixed patterns react to every step you take.

## Rights

Penguin Software became Polarware and wound down in the early 1990s. The
Macintosh Pensate has not been sold in decades and is in no current product.

Polarware's founders have at times released parts of the old catalogue
publicly; no such release covering this build has been found, and none is
claimed here.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `Pensate4amMacCrack` — a Macintosh floppy, copy
protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
