# Greebles (Stairways Shareware, 1997) — Macintosh

## What this is

Greebles by Peter N Lewis, published through Stairways Shareware — a
Macintosh puzzle game with custom levels and network play.

## Licence

Shareware, distributed freely by its author through Stairways. The copy here
is unregistered and unmodified, with the registration notice and the
instructions intact.

Peter N Lewis and Stairways have a long record of releasing their back
catalogue freely; no specific grant covering this build has been located, so
it rests on the ordinary shareware distribution model rather than on a
particular permission.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `tucows_205596_Greebles`, from the Tucows Macintosh
archive. The `.sit` expands to a plain folder, copied onto a small title disk
that mounts beside the shared Mac OS 8.6 image.
