# MacMatch (Axlon, 1984) — Macintosh

## What this is

MacMatch, published by Axlon in conjunction with The Software Machine — an
early Macintosh memory and matching game, credited on its title screen to
Eagle I. Berns, Michael Kosaka and Phillip Goldman.

## Rights

Axlon was Nolan Bushnell's company and is long gone; its software catalogue
was never transferred to an identifiable successor. MacMatch has not been sold
since the mid-1980s.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `MacMatch4amCrack` — a Macintosh floppy, copy protection
removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
