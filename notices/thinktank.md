# ThinkTank 128 (Living Videotext, 1984) — Macintosh

## What this is

ThinkTank 128 v1.000, Dave Winer's outline processor for the original
Macintosh — the program that introduced most people to the idea of an outliner
as a category of software.

## Rights

Living Videotext merged into Symantec in 1987. Symantec still exists; ThinkTank
does not, and has not been sold in roughly thirty-five years.

Dave Winer has written extensively about this software's history and has
released some later related work publicly. No permission covering this
Macintosh build has been found, and none is claimed.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `ThinkTank4amMacCrack` — a Macintosh floppy, copy
protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
