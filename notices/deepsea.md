# Deep Sea Classic (Peter Wagner, 1997) — Macintosh

## What this is

Deep Sea Classic — an undersea Macintosh action game. On startup it shows its
registration screen, which says plainly that Deep Sea is shareware and that
the copy is unregistered, with a "Try Out" button that plays the game.

That notice is the author's and is left exactly where it is.

## Licence

Shareware, distributed freely with a registration request and a try-out mode.
The copy here is unregistered and unmodified.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `tucows_205594_DeepSeaClassic`, from the Tucows
Macintosh archive. The `.sit` expands to a plain folder, copied onto a title
disk that mounts beside the shared Mac OS 8.6 image.
