# Speller Bee (First Byte, 1986) — Macintosh

## What this is

Speller Bee by First Byte — a spelling program for children that speaks the
words aloud using the company's own speech synthesiser, with a cartoon bee for
company.

## Rights

First Byte was a speech-technology company that published a small line of
talking educational software in the mid-1980s and no longer exists. Speller Bee
has not been sold in decades and no successor product contains it.

Its sibling SmoothTalker, from the same publisher, is **not** on this site: the
surviving copy still demands its original disk on startup, so there is nothing
playable to host.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `SpellerBee4amMacCrack` — a Macintosh floppy, copy
protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
