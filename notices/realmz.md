# Realmz (Fantasoft, 1994-1999) — Macintosh

## What this is

Realmz 8.0.5, Tim Phillips's party-based role-playing game published by
Fantasoft — the engine plus the scenarios that shipped with this release.

## Licence

Shareware. Realmz was distributed freely with its scenarios playable and
registration asked for by the author; the copy here is unregistered and
unmodified, with the registration notices left exactly where they are.

Fantasoft wound down in the 2000s and Realmz has not been sold since. Tim
Phillips has at times spoken publicly about the game's later status, but no
specific grant covering redistribution has been located, so this rests on the
ordinary shareware model rather than on a particular permission.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `tucows_205562_Realmz`, from the Tucows Macintosh
archive. The `.sit` expands to a plain folder, copied onto a 90 MB title disk
that mounts beside the shared Mac OS 8.6 image.
