# Diamonds 3D (Varcon Systems, 1998) — Macintosh

## What this is

**The demo build** of Diamonds 3D, published by Varcon Systems — the
brick-breaking puzzle game descended from the original Diamonds. This is the
copy Varcon distributed free; the full game was sold.

That it is the demo is said here and on its page rather than glossed over.

It runs only in 256-colour mode and offers to change the monitor's depth for
you on startup.

## Licence

A freely distributed demo. Varcon Systems distributed this build at no charge
to advertise the paid version, which is exactly what is being done with it
here, and it is unregistered and unmodified.

Varcon Systems is no longer trading and Diamonds 3D has not been sold in
around two decades.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `tucows_205678_Diamonds_3D`, from the Tucows Macintosh
archive. The `.sit` expands to a plain folder, copied onto a title disk that
mounts beside the shared Mac OS 8.6 image.
