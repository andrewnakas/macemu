# Wizardry II: The Knight of Diamonds (Sir-Tech, 1986) — Macintosh

## What this is

Version 4.0 of the Macintosh Wizardry II: The Knight of Diamonds — the first
Wizardry scenario disk, six levels, designed for a party that has already
finished Proving Grounds.

## Rights

The same position as Wizardry I, set out in that title's notice: Sir-Tech
closed in 2003, the rights moved on, and a commercial remake of the *first*
game is on sale today from Digital Eclipse.

No remake or re-release of Knight of Diamonds is currently sold, and this
1986 Macintosh build is in no current product. As with the first game, if any
rightsholder objects this comes down at once.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `WizardryII_v40_MacSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy
with its copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
