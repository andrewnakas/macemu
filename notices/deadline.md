# Deadline (Infocom, 1982/1984) — Macintosh

## What this is

Release 27 of Deadline, Marc Blank's murder mystery — the first Infocom game
that was not straight fantasy or science fiction — on its Macintosh floppy.

Deadline shipped with a folder of physical evidence: the coroner's report,
interview transcripts, photographs, a scrap of paper from the study. Those are
not on the disk and were never on the disk. The game is playable without them
and considerably harder, which is worth knowing before you start.

## Rights

Activision owns Infocom's catalogue and still exists.

Deadline has not been sold since "Classic Text Adventure Masterpieces of
Infocom" went out of print in the late 1990s, and Activision has not made it
available in any form since. It is not part of any current product.

The Zork trilogy is deliberately **not** on this site, because it is sold today
as the Zork Anthology. The line is what is purchasable now, not what is famous.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `Deadline4amMacCrack` — a 400K Macintosh floppy, copy
protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
