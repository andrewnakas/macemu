# Math Blaster (Davidson & Associates, 1986) — Macintosh

## What this is

Version 1.0 of the Macintosh Math Blaster, with all six topics, the problem
editor, and the saved student records that were on the original disk.

## Rights

Jan and Bob Davidson founded Davidson & Associates in 1982 and sold it to CUC
International in 1996 for roughly a billion dollars. The catalogue became part
of Knowledge Adventure, and Math Blaster products are sold today.

Those are new software — different engines, different artwork, decades of
sequels between them and this. The 1986 Macintosh build has not been sold by
anyone in nearly forty years.

## A note on the student records

The disk carries saved progress for three children named Zachary, Lou Anne and
Jeremy. They are the records of whoever owned this copy in the 1980s, they
contain nothing but scores against arithmetic topics, and they have been left
where they were because removing them would be editing the artefact.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `MathBlaster4amMacCrack` — a 400K MFS floppy,
copy-protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am. The application and
its 38 data files were copied onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk; the floppy's own
System and Finder were dropped and its ImageWriter driver filed into the System
Folder.
