# OrbQuest (Highland Computer Services, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

OrbQuest v1.04, an early Macintosh dungeon role-playing game: you create a
character, go underground, and look for the seven scattered Wards of the Orb.

## Rights

Highland Computer Services does not appear to exist in any form today, and
OrbQuest has not been sold by anyone for roughly forty years. There is no
successor product and no reachable claimant that research has turned up.

This is about as close to a genuinely abandoned work as this catalogue gets.
"Nobody has claimed it" is still not the same as "nobody owns it", and it is
not asserted here to be.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `OrbQuestSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy with its copy
protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
