# MacCheckers and Reversi (Videx, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

MacCheckers and Reversi, published by Videx — two board games in one
application, with a move timer and selectable skill levels.

## Rights

Videx was an Apple-peripheral company that stopped publishing software long
ago. This title has not been sold since the 1980s and no successor product
contains it.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `MacCheckersAndReversi4amMacCrack` — a Macintosh floppy,
copy protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
