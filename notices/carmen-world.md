# Where in the World is Carmen Sandiego? (Brøderbund, 1985–1991) — Macintosh

## What this is

Version 1.3 of the Macintosh *Where in the World is Carmen Sandiego?* — the
original and best-known of the series, credited on its title screen to Gene
Portwood, Lauren Elliott and Chris Jochumson, with graphics by Don Albrecht
and Michelle McBride.

The World Almanac that shipped in the box is not here. The game was sold with
one and expects you to look things up in it; without it you are relying on
what the game tells you and on your own geography.

## Rights

The same position as *Where in Europe is Carmen Sandiego?*, already on this
site, and stated the same way.

Brøderbund was acquired by The Learning Company in 1998, and Carmen Sandiego
now belongs to Houghton Mifflin Harcourt, who have licensed it actively — a
Netflix animated series in 2019 and new games alongside it.

Those are new software. This 1991 Macintosh build has not been sold by anyone
in over thirty years and is contained in no current product. The argument is
about this specific artefact being out of commerce, not about the name being
free, and the name plainly is not.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `where-in-the-world-is-carmen-sandiego-mac-en`, which
ships the game as a zip rather than a raw disk image. It was unpacked by hand;
inside is an 800K DiskCopy 4.2 image of the v1.3 floppy, and that is what the
application and its data were taken from and copied onto a System 7.5.3 boot
disk.
