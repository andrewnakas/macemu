# Where in Europe Is Carmen Sandiego? (Broderbund, 1989) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of the Europe edition, complete with the graphics file
that shipped on its second floppy.

## Rights

Broderbund was acquired by The Learning Company in 1998, and Carmen Sandiego now
belongs to Houghton Mifflin Harcourt, who have licensed it actively: a Netflix
animated series in 2019, and new games alongside it.

Those are new software. The 1989 Macintosh build has not been sold by anyone in
over thirty years and is contained in no current product.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `WhereInEuropeIsCarmenSandiego4amMacCrack` — two preserved
Macintosh floppies, copy-protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

Both disks matter. The application and its music file are on the first; the
second holds nothing but `Carmen Europe Graphics`, 653K of it. A copy built from
the first disk alone starts and immediately reports that the graphics file
cannot be found, which is a common state for this title in circulation. Both are
present here.
