# Colibricks (Yann Bonnamy, 1999) — Macintosh

## What this is

Colibricks by Yann Bonnamy — a colour brick-breaker for the Macintosh with
prize balloons, special bricks, archer bricks and a laser.

## Licence

Shareware, distributed freely by its author with a registration menu built in.
The copy here is unregistered and unmodified.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `tucows_205734_Colibricks`, from the Tucows Macintosh
archive. The `.sit` expands to a plain folder; the Classic build was copied
onto a title disk that mounts beside the shared Mac OS 8.6 image.
