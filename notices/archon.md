# Archon: The Light and the Dark (Electronic Arts, 1984) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of Archon — the full game, both sides, all the spell
squares, on the original 400K floppy it shipped on.

## Rights

Archon was written by Free Fall Associates — Paul Reiche III, Anne Westfall
and Jon Freeman — and published by Electronic Arts in 1983 for the Atari 800
and Apple II, then ported to the Macintosh in 1984.

Electronic Arts still exists and still owns it. That is worth stating plainly
rather than burying: this is not an orphan work, and the argument for hosting
it is narrower than that.

The argument is that this specific artefact is out of commerce and has been for
about forty years. EA has never re-released the Macintosh build. Archon has
appeared since in other forms — a 2008 remake, compilation discs in the 1990s —
but none of them contain this program, and none of them has been purchasable
for a long time either. There is no product anywhere that a person could buy
that would get them the thing on this page.

That is an abandonment argument, not a licence. It is offered honestly as such.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `ArchonMacSanIncCrack` — a 400K MFS floppy, copy
protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk is served as it is, with its own System on it, booting on an emulated
Macintosh Plus. The System 7.5.3 route used for most titles here does not work
for Archon: the application is from 1984, predates almost every Toolbox call
System 7 expects an application to make, and resets the machine on launch. Its
own System is the only one it runs under.

The floppy's startup program is the application itself rather than the Finder,
which is why the game appears without a desktop ever being drawn.
