# Harrier Strike Mission (Miles Computing, 1986) — Macintosh

## What this is

Harrier Strike Mission, Miles Computing's Harrier flight and combat simulator
for the Macintosh, with a full instrument panel under the view.

## Rights

Miles Computing left the games business at the end of the 1980s and the
company no longer publishes. Harrier Strike Mission has not been sold in
roughly forty years and is in no current product.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `HarrierStrikeMissionSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy
with its copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

This disk is served with its own System and its **original boot arrangement
left alone**. The Miles Computing disks do not carry a launchable application
in the ordinary sense — the only APPL on the volume is a "Reset & 'Boot' disk"
stub, and the game is started by the disk's own boot blocks. Naming any file as
a startup program breaks it; not touching it works.
