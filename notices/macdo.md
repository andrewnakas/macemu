# MacDo (Macintosh shareware, 1998)

## What this is

MacDo 1.0.4 — a Macintosh puzzle game that opens by asking whether you want
English, German or Swedish.

## Licence

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `tucows_205697_MacDo`, from the Tucows Macintosh
archive. The `.sit` expands to a plain folder, copied onto a title disk that
mounts beside the shared Mac OS 8.6 image.
