# Pipe Dream (LucasArts, 1990) — Macintosh

## What this is

Version 1.2 of the Macintosh Pipe Dream, with the high-score table, Trainer
mode and the two-player mode.

## Rights

The Assembly Line wrote it and released it in Europe as Pipe Mania in 1989;
LucasArts published it in the United States as Pipe Dream. The concept has been
remade many times since — Pipe Mania re-releases, and an enormous number of
minigames that copy it outright — and some of those are current products.

None of them is this build, which no one has sold in over thirty years. The
underlying idea is not protectable and has been reimplemented freely for
decades; what is hosted here is the specific 1990 Macintosh program.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `PipeDream4amMacCrack` — preserved Macintosh floppies,
copy-protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am. The application,
its desk accessory and its music file were copied onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk.
The Read Me was kept out of the startup folder and left on the desktop, so that
the Finder does not open it on top of the game.
