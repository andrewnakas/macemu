# The Oregon Trail (MECC) — Macintosh

## What this is

MECC's Macintosh release of The Oregon Trail, preserved as a 5 MB hard-disk
image and run here on an emulated Macintosh II.

## Rights

MECC — the Minnesota Educational Computing Consortium — began as a state
agency, was privatised in 1991, sold to SoftKey in 1995, folded into The
Learning Company, bought by Mattel in 1999, sold to Gores Technology in 2000,
and the education catalogue eventually reached Houghton Mifflin Harcourt.

HMH has licensed the name since: a 2021 mobile and Nintendo Switch remake, and
a card game. Those are new software built from nothing but the name and the
premise. No rights holder has sold the Macintosh release in more than
twenty-five years, and no current product contains it.

## Why it is hosted

This is the single most-remembered piece of educational software ever made, and
the Macintosh build is the one most people actually played, because it is what
the school computer labs of the 1990s had. It has been out of commerce for a
quarter of a century and is otherwise available only as an abandonware download
or a video of someone else playing it.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `OregonTrailMacintosh`. The application and its data files
were copied into a System 7.5.3 boot disk; the disk's own System Folder was left
behind, since the boot disk supplies a better one.

A note on the version: this build uses a small palette, so the artwork is close
to two-tone on a colour screen. That is the build, not the emulation. It runs on
an emulated Macintosh II rather than a compact Mac because it is happy with a
colour screen and looks better on one.
