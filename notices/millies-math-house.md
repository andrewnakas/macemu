# Millie's Math House (Edmark, 1995) — Macintosh CD

**Hosted:** yes, as `millies-math-house-title`, mounted next to the shared Mac OS 8.6
system.

## What this is

Millie's Math House for Macintosh, version 2.0, © 1994–1995 Edmark Corp., from Edmark's Early Learning House
series for children aged about two to six. It was developed by Theatrix
Interactive and published by Edmark Corporation of Redmond, Washington.

## Rights

Edmark was bought by IBM in 1996, sold to Riverdeep in 2000 and passed with
Riverdeep into Houghton Mifflin Harcourt, which closed the Edmark brand in 2017
and sold rights to many of its products to Rise Global in 2021. This 1990s
CD release has not been sold for more than twenty years; the last reissue of
the House series was Riverdeep's own 2007 classroom edition, itself long out of
print. That is a position about this out-of-commerce release, not a claim that
it is free of copyright; the rights holders' rights are acknowledged.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `millies-math-house`, file `Millie's Math House (USA).7z`: a redump bin/cue of a hybrid
Windows/Macintosh disc with an Apple partition map, converted to 2048-byte
sectors before reading.

The application refuses to start unless its data folder sits beside it, so the
application and its `MMH Data` folder were copied, unaltered, from the disc's
Macintosh volume onto the title disk. The Edmark demo, the parents' guide
films, the registration program and the disc's own copy of QuickTime were left
behind. The application file is named "Start App" (with an alias, "Start") so
the shared system can launch it at boot. Only its name changed.

Played on 2026-10-05: played the main room and the Cookie Factory.
