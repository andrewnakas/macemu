# Mouse Stampede (Mark of the Unicorn, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

Mouse Stampede v1.00, an early Macintosh arcade game from Mark of the Unicorn
— mice overrun the screen and you deal with them.

## Rights

Mark of the Unicorn still exists, as MOTU, but as a music-hardware and
software company; its early 1980s Macintosh games line was abandoned decades
ago and nothing containing this program has been sold since.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `MouseStampede4amMacCrack` — a Macintosh floppy, copy
protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
