# Crystal Quest (Casady & Greene, 1987) — Macintosh

## What this is

Version 2.2.5 of the Macintosh Crystal Quest, written by Patrick Buckland and
published by Casady & Greene.

## Rights

Casady & Greene shut down in 2003 and its catalogue was not sold on as a whole;
several of its titles were returned to their individual authors at the time.

A Crystal Quest remake was released on Xbox Live Arcade in 2007, developed by
Frozen Codebase. It is a new program that shares the name and the premise. The
Macintosh original has not been sold in over twenty years.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `CrystalQuest_2_2_5M` — a preserved 1.4 MB Macintosh
disk. The application, its sound files and its sound sets were copied onto a
System 7.5.3 boot disk.

The disk also carries CritterEditor, a bundled tool larger than the game
itself, which is why the application had to be named explicitly when the disk
was imported rather than picked by size. The Read Me was kept out of the
startup folder so the Finder does not open it on top of the game.
