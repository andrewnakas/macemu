# Murder by the Dozen (Thunder Mountain, 1986) — Macintosh

## What this is

Murder by the Dozen, a collection of twelve mystery cases presented through a
"Mystery Master" front end: you pick a case, follow leads around a town map,
and name a suspect when you think you can prove it.

## Rights

Thunder Mountain was Mindscape's budget label. Mindscape's assets were
dispersed after the company wound down in the 2000s, and this title has not
been sold by anyone since the 1980s.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `MurderByTheDozen4amMacCrack` — a Macintosh floppy, copy
protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
