# Wizardry: Proving Grounds of the Mad Overlord (Sir-Tech, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of the first Wizardry — Andrew Greenberg and Robert
Woodhead's dungeon crawl, with the party roster, the training grounds and the
ten-level maze.

## Rights

This one needs care, because the name is current and the artefact is not.

Sir-Tech Software closed in 2003. The Wizardry rights have since sat with
Japanese holders, and in 2023–24 Digital Eclipse released *Wizardry: Proving
Grounds of the Mad Overlord* as a new commercial remake, which is sold today.

That remake is new software. It is not this program, and buying it does not
get you this one. The 1985 Macintosh build has not been sold by anybody in
decades and is in no current product.

Even so, a remake of the same title being actively sold puts this closer to
the line than most of the catalogue. It is hosted on the out-of-commerce
argument alone, and **if any current rightsholder objects it comes down at
once** — the existence of a paid modern version makes that the only defensible
posture.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `WizardrySanIncCrack` — version 1.10 on a Macintosh
floppy, copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The floppy's own System produces a Sad Mac on an emulated Macintosh Plus, so
the application was copied onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk, where it runs.
