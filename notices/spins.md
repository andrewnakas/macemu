# Spins! (The Foundation Company, 1999) — Macintosh

## What this is

Spins! v1.2, published by The Foundation Company — a Macintosh puzzle game.
Its splash screen carries the company's logo and web address.

## Licence

Shareware, distributed freely. The copy here is unregistered and unmodified.

The Foundation Company appears to have stopped trading; the web address on
the splash screen no longer resolves to them. No successor product contains
this program.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `tucows_205702_Spins`, from the Tucows Macintosh
archive. The `.sit` expands to a plain folder, copied onto a title disk that
mounts beside the shared Mac OS 8.6 image.
