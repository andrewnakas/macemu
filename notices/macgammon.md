# MacGammon! (Videx, 1984) — Macintosh

## What this is

MacGammon!, a backgammon program for the Macintosh, copyright 1984 Thomas C.
Hoffman and Steven W. Slesinger, published by Videx.

## Rights

Videx stopped publishing software long ago and the named authors have not
offered the program since. It has not been sold in about forty years.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `MacGammon4amMacCrack` — a Macintosh floppy, copy
protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
