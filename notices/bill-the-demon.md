# Bill the Demon (Bryan Smith, 1997) — Macintosh

## What this is

Bill the Demon by Bryan Smith — a Macintosh action game that runs in 256-colour
mode and offers to switch your monitor's depth for you on startup, restoring it
when you quit.

A black-and-white version existed separately; this is the colour one.
