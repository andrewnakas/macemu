# Seven Cities of Gold (Electronic Arts, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of Ozark Softscape's Seven Cities of Gold — Dan Bunten's
game of sailing from Spain to a procedurally generated New World and doing
whatever you decide to do when you get there.

## Rights

Electronic Arts published it and still exists. Not an orphan work, and not
described as one.

The 1985 Macintosh build is out of commerce and has been for about forty years.
EA has not re-released it. A 1993 remake, Seven Cities of Gold: Commemorative
Edition, was a different program on different hardware and is itself long out
of print. Nothing currently purchasable contains this disk.

Dan Bunten later transitioned and was known as Danielle Bunten Berry; the 1985
packaging carries the earlier name, which is why both appear in writing about
the game.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `SevenCitiesOfGoldSanIncCrack` — a 400K Macintosh floppy
with its copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
