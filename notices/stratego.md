# Stratego (Accolade, 1988) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh Stratego, published by Accolade under licence from the
boardgame's owner — the full board, the terrain, and the piece palette.

## Rights

Two layers here, and both are live.

The **Stratego boardgame** is a current product. It is owned by Jumbo
(Koninklijke Jumbo B.V.) and sold in shops today. Nothing on this page is a
claim on that; a boardgame and a 1988 computer adaptation of it are different
things, and buying one has never got you the other.

The **software** was Accolade's, and Accolade's catalogue is now with Atari SA.
This particular Macintosh program has not been sold in about thirty-five years
and is in no current product.

Because the underlying property is actively sold, this sits closer to the line
than most of the catalogue. If Jumbo or Atari object it comes down at once.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `Stratego4amMacCrack` — a Macintosh floppy, copy
protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am. The floppy carries no
System of its own, so the application was copied onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk.
