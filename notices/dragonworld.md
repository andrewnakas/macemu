# Dragonworld (Trillium, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

Dragonworld, Trillium's illustrated adventure based on the novel by Byron
Preiss and Michael Reaves, with character portraits and a text parser.

## Rights

Trillium became Telarium, a Spinnaker Software imprint. Spinnaker's assets
passed through several hands in the 1990s and the Telarium catalogue has not
been published by anyone since.

There is a second layer here: the game adapts a novel, and the book rights are
separate from the software rights and rest with the authors' estates and
publishers. Nothing about hosting this disk touches the novel.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `Dragonworld4amMacCrack` — a Macintosh floppy, copy
protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
