# Sapiens (Didier & Olivier Guillion, 1997) — Macintosh

## What this is

Sapiens by Didier and Olivier Guillion of Toulouse — a Macintosh strategy
game. It opens by asking which language you want: English Version, or Version
Française.

## Licence

Shareware, distributed freely by its authors, whose names, postal address and
email are on the opening screen exactly as they put them there. The copy here
is unregistered and unmodified.

The Guillions went on to found Myriad Software, which still trades — the email
address on the title screen is a myriad-online.com one. They are reachable,
which makes this an easy takedown to honour if they want it.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `tucows_205622_Sapiens`, from the Tucows Macintosh
archive. The `.sit` expands to a plain folder, copied onto a title disk that
mounts beside the shared Mac OS 8.6 image.
