# Sierra Championship Boxing (Sierra On-Line, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

Sierra Championship Boxing for the Macintosh, by Dave Murry and Barry Murry,
copyright 1985 Sierra On-Line. You manage and fight boxers, with a ringside
view and a ticket-stub title screen.

## Rights

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `SierraChampionshipBoxing4amMacCrack` — a Macintosh
floppy, copy protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus. The
application is named "SIERRA CHAMPIONSHIP BOXING"; the boot blocks hold fifteen
characters for the name of the startup program, so on this copy the file is
called "SIERRA CHAMPION". Nothing else was changed.
