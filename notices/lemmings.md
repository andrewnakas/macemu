# Lemmings (Psygnosis, 1991) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh port of Lemmings, in colour, with the full level set.

## Rights

DMA Design created Lemmings in Dundee and Psygnosis published it. Psygnosis was
bought by Sony in 1993 and DMA Design became Rockstar North. Sony has published
Lemmings re-releases on its own platforms, including a PS3 version and mobile
releases, so the name is not dormant.

Those are new software built for new hardware. The 1991 Macintosh port has not
been sold by anyone in over thirty years and is contained in no current product.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `mac_lemmings_1` — a preserved 10 MB Macintosh disk. The
application and its data were copied onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk.
