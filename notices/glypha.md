# Glypha III — provenance and licence

**Hosted:** yes, as `glypha-v1`.
**Provenance:** released as freeware and open source by its author.

## Where the files came from

The MacSilverWare shareware CD-ROM at the Internet Archive
([`macsilverware`](https://archive.org/details/macsilverware)), at
`Games:Arcade:Glypha 3.0`. Copied into a System 7.5.3 boot image by
`scripts/build-title-image.py`, resource fork and Finder metadata intact.

## Why it can be here

John Calhoun published Glypha's complete source code at
[softdorothy/Glypha3](https://github.com/softdorothy/Glypha3), alongside his
other Macintosh games, and the game was distributed as freeware. He wrote it;
Soft Dorothy Software was his own imprint, so there is no publisher whose
consent is in question.

That release is why a modern port exists —
[kainjow/Glypha](https://github.com/kainjow/Glypha) rebuilds it for current
systems — and it puts this title on the same footing as Glider PRO: free
because the person who made it decided it should be.
