# Sargon III (Hayden Software, 1984) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of Sargon III, the third of Dan and Kathe Spracklen's
chess programs — the one rewritten around a proper search rather than the
pattern tables of the first two.

## Rights

Hayden Software is long gone. Sargon later passed through Activision, which
published Sargon 4 and 5, and the line stopped in the 1990s.

Nobody currently sells Sargon III in any form, on any platform. The rights are
not obviously held by anyone reachable, and the artefact has been out of
commerce for around forty years. This is closer to a genuinely abandoned work
than most of the catalogue, though "nobody has claimed it" is not the same as
"nobody owns it", and it is not claimed here to be.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `SargonIIIMacSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy with its
copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
