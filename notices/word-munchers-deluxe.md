# Word Munchers Deluxe (MECC, 1996) — Macintosh CD

**Hosted:** yes, as `word-munchers-deluxe-title` (the application and its
small data files) beside `word-munchers-deluxe-cd` (the disc's Macintosh
volume), mounted next to the shared Mac OS 8.6 system.

## What this is

Version 1.0 of Word Munchers Deluxe for Macintosh, © 1996 MECC, from a 1997
hybrid Windows/Macintosh CD-ROM. It is the CD-era remake of MECC's 1985 Word
Munchers: the same grid of words to eat and Troggles to dodge, with grade 1–5
skill sets in classification, grammar, phonics, vocabulary and sentences.

## Rights

MECC was sold to SoftKey in 1995 and its catalogue reached Houghton Mifflin
Harcourt by way of The Learning Company and Mattel. Word Munchers Deluxe has
not been sold for more than twenty years and no current product contains it.
That is a position about this out-of-commerce release, not a claim that it is
free of copyright; the rights holders' rights are acknowledged.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `word-munchers-deluxe`, file
`Word Munchers Deluxe (1997)(MECC)[Mac-PC].iso`: a hybrid disc with an Apple
partition map.

- **Title disk.** The disc's installer copies the application and its small
  "disk" data files to the hard disk and reads the large "cd" files from the
  disc. The title disk does the same by hand: the application (fat 68k and
  PowerPC), `WMPdisk.PIC`, `WMSdisk.SND`, `WMUNCHER.MMS` and the Read Me, all
  copied unaltered. The application file is named "Start App" (with an alias,
  "Start") so the shared system can launch it at boot. Only its name changed.
- **CD.** The Macintosh volume ("Word Munchers Deluxe CD") is served byte for
  byte as it sits in the disc's Apple_HFS partition. It also carries MECC's own
  demo programs and an AOL installer, which are left as they were and are not
  launched or linked to.

## Why Mac OS 8.6

The Read Me asks for a 68040 or a Power Macintosh, so it runs under the shared
Mac OS 8.6 image on an emulated Power Macintosh (SheepShaver) rather than on
the 68020 Macintosh II.

Played on 2026-10-05: the title screen, Play with Troggles, the grade 1
Classification book, and a level 1 board of "red things", munched with the
arrow keys and space bar.
