# The King of Chicago (Cinemaware, 1986) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of The King of Chicago, designed by Doug Sharp and
published by Cinemaware through Master Designer Software — a Prohibition-era
story told as a branching film, with the player choosing what the character
thinks and says.

## Rights

Cinemaware was wound up in 2014 and the trademark and catalogue have moved
through several hands since. Various Cinemaware re-releases and compilations
have appeared over the years on other platforms; none of them is the 1986
Macintosh build and none contains it.

As with Defender of the Crown, this is among the less settled rights positions
on this site. It is hosted because the specific artefact has not been offered
for sale by Cinemaware or any successor in more than thirty years. **If anyone
with a colourable claim asks, it comes down without argument.**

Doug Sharp has written publicly about the game's development over the years;
nothing here is presented as permission from him.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `TheKingOfChicagoSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy with
its copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The floppy carries a MiniFinder as well as the game, and on its own System the
machine reaches the launcher rather than the game whatever the boot blocks say.
The application was therefore copied onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk, where it
starts correctly; that is why a desktop appears briefly first.
