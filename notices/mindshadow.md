# Mindshadow (Activision, 1984) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of Mindshadow, developed by Interplay for Activision —
Interplay's first published game. You wake on a beach with amnesia and the
whole game is the recovery of who you are.

The copy opens on Interplay's own splash, offering the game or a tutorial.

## Rights

Activision published it and still exists; Interplay developed it and also still
exists. Which of them could act on the Macintosh build is not something this
site can determine.

Neither has sold it in decades, and it is not part of any current product from
either company.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `MindshadowSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy with its
copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
