# Shufflepuck Café — provenance and licence

**Hosted:** yes, as `shufflepuck-v3`.
**Provenance:** abandoned commercial software.

## Where the files came from

The [`ShufflepuckCafe4amMacCrack`](https://archive.org/details/ShufflepuckCafe4amMacCrack)
item at the Internet Archive, which preserves the game as DiskCopy 4.2 floppy
images. Disk two holds the application; the DiskCopy header was stripped and
the application copied into a System 7.5.3 boot image by
`scripts/build-title-image.py`, resource fork intact.

The preserved copy is a 4am crack — the copy protection has been removed, which
is what makes it runnable from a disk image at all. The original shipped on a
key-disk-protected floppy that expected to be read on real hardware.

## Why it is here

Abandoned, and the page says so rather than reaching for a licence that does
not exist.

Shufflepuck Café was published by Brøderbund in 1988 and written by Christopher
Gross, Lauren Elliott and Gene Portwood. Brøderbund was acquired by The Learning
Company in 1998, which passed through Mattel, Gores Technology and Riverdeep;
the catalogue is now nominally Houghton Mifflin Harcourt's. Nobody has sold this
title in roughly thirty-five years and no rights holder distributes it.

It is preserved here on the same practical footing as the Internet Archive's
software collections: not commercially available, not otherwise obtainable,
worth keeping runnable. That is a position rather than a legal claim.
