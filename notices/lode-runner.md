# Lode Runner (Broderbund, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

Version 1.0 of the Macintosh release of Lode Runner, with all 150 levels and
the level editor.

## Rights

Doug Smith wrote Lode Runner and sold it to Broderbund in 1983. The property
now belongs to Tozai Games, who have kept it alive: Lode Runner Legacy, Lode
Runner 1 and others are current products, sold today.

Those are new software. They are remakes with new engines, new artwork and new
levels, and buying one does not get you this. The 1985 Macintosh build has not
been sold by Broderbund, Tozai or anyone else in forty years, and no current
product contains it.

That is a narrower argument than the one for a title whose owner has vanished,
and it is made deliberately: this is hosted because the specific artefact is
out of commerce, not because the name is.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `LodeRunner_v10_4amMacCrack` — a 400K MFS floppy,
copy-protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am. The application
and its data were copied onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk.
