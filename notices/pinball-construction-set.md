# Pinball Construction Set (Electronic Arts, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

Bill Budge's Pinball Construction Set for the Macintosh. It is a pinball game
only in the sense that a word processor is a novel: you drag bumpers, flippers
and targets onto a blank table, set the physics, and play what you built.

## Rights

Electronic Arts published it and still exists.

The Macintosh build is from 1985 and has never been re-released by EA in any
form. It is not part of any current product and cannot be bought. That is the
whole of the argument for it being here — an out-of-commerce artefact, not an
ownerless one.

## Why it matters

Pinball Construction Set is one of the first pieces of consumer software to
make direct manipulation the entire interface: no modes, no command language,
just objects you pick up and put down. It was widely cited as an influence on
the software that followed it, and it is a reasonable claim that it is the
first mass-market "construction set" program of any kind.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `PinballConstructionSetSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy
with its copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
