# Tass Times in Tonetown (Activision, 1986) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of Tass Times in Tonetown, designed by Michael Berlyn and
Muffy McClung Berlyn and developed by Interplay for Activision. It is a
graphic adventure with a compass, an inventory strip and a parser, set in a
world where fashion is the local physics.

## Rights

Activision published it and still exists. Interplay, which developed it, also
still exists; which of them could act on the Macintosh build today is not
something this site can determine.

What is clear is that it has not been sold in any form for decades and is not
part of a current product from either company.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `TassTimesInTonetownSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy
with its copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
