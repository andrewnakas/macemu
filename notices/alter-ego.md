# Alter Ego (Activision, 1986) — Macintosh

## What this is

The male edition of Alter Ego, Peter J. Favaro's life simulation, on its
Macintosh floppy. Activision released male and female editions with different
content; this is the male one, which is what the archive holds.

## Rights

Activision published it and still exists. It has not been sold since the
1980s and is part of no current Activision product.

A free browser reimplementation of Alter Ego has circulated for years with
Favaro's involvement. That is a separate piece of software and confers no
permission for this disk; it is mentioned only because people reasonably
confuse the two.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `AlterEgoMaleSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy with its
copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
