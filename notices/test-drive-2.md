# The Duel: Test Drive II (Accolade, 1989) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of The Duel: Test Drive II — the Ferrari F40 against the
Porsche 959, on open road, with police.

The expansion disks Accolade sold separately (California Challenge, European
Challenge, the Supercars and Muscle Cars packs) are not here. This is the base
game as it shipped.

## Rights

Accolade was acquired by Infogrames in 1999 and the catalogue is now with
Atari SA. The company that owns it exists and is reachable.

Test Drive as a brand has continued — Test Drive Unlimited and its sequels are
recent products. None of them contains the 1989 Macintosh build, and no
storefront sells it. As with Chessmaster, a live brand does not make the old
artefact purchasable.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `TheDuelTestDriveIISanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy with
its copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
