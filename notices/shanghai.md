# Shanghai (Activision, 1986) — Macintosh

## What this is

The 1986 Macintosh release of Shanghai, written by Brodie Lockard and published
by Activision. It boots from a preserved copy of its own floppy.

## Rights

Activision still exists, and Shanghai-branded games have been published since —
Shanghai II, Shanghai: Second Dynasty, and various handheld and mobile versions,
mostly through Sunsoft under licence. None of them is this. The 1986 Macintosh
build has not been distributed by anyone in nearly forty years.

The tile layout Shanghai introduced is not protectable and has been reimplemented
thousands of times, freely and commercially. What is hosted here is the original
program, not the idea.

## Why it matters enough to host

Mahjong solitaire is widely assumed to be an ancient game. It is not: it is a
1986 computer game with a named author, who designed it while recovering from an
injury that had left him almost completely paralysed. The original being
playable is the clearest possible correction to a very common misunderstanding.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `ShanghaiSanIncCrack` — a 400K MFS floppy, copy-protection
removed by the preservationist known as san inc. The image is used as it is: the
disk names Shanghai as the startup program, so it boots directly into the game.
The 1986 application does not run under System 7 at all.
