# Solitaire Till Dawn (Semicolon Software, 2001) — Macintosh

## What this is

Solitaire Till Dawn 4.0.1 by Semicolon Software — a collection of solitaire
card games with a shared engine, statistics and a game guide.

**This copy is the free demo.** On startup it says so plainly: "FREE DEMO —
100 GAMES. You may play up to 100 free games with this version. Purchasing a
registration key will completely remove all restrictions." That limit is the
author's and is left intact; nothing has been patched.

## Licence

Shareware, distributed freely in this demo form by its authors. Semicolon
Software's Solitaire Till Dawn was later sold commercially for Mac OS X, and
a successor product has existed under that name — which is why the position is
stated carefully here.

What is hosted is the **classic Mac OS 4.0.1 demo build**, exactly as it was
distributed for free, with its own restriction notice showing. No current
product contains this program, and the demo was always meant to be passed
around.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `tucows_205757_Solitaire_Till_Dawn`, from the Tucows
