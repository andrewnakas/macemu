# Pyramid of Peril (Aegis Development, 1985) — Macintosh

**Hosted:** yes, as `pyramid-of-peril-v1`.

## Rights

Aegis Development closed long ago and Pyramid of Peril has not been sold since
the 1980s. It is preserved here on the same practical footing as the rest of
the 1984–86 Macintosh catalogue on this site: a position, not a legal claim.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `mac_Pyramid_of_Peril_v1`, file
`Pyramid_of_Peril_v1.dsk`: a 400K MFS floppy with its own System. The disk
boots on its own System (`--use-source-system`), with the boot blocks set to
launch "Pyramid Game" directly, because 1985 applications often crash later
system software. No copy protection check appears.

Played on 2026-09-27: boots into the first corridor with the movement buttons
working.
