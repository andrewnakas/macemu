# Hacker (Activision, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of Hacker, written by Steve Cartwright for Activision.

It opens with a logon prompt and no instructions of any kind. That is not
missing documentation — the game shipped that way deliberately, and the box
told you almost nothing either. Working out what you are looking at is the
first puzzle.

## Rights

Activision published it and still exists.

Hacker has not been sold since the 1980s. It appeared on some Activision
compilation discs in the 1990s, all long out of print, and no current
Activision product contains the Macintosh build. It is out of commerce rather
than ownerless, and that is the basis on which it is here.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `HackerMacSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy with its copy
protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
