# Balance of Power (Mindscape, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

Chris Crawford's Balance of Power for the Macintosh — the geopolitical
simulation in which the United States and the Soviet Union compete for
prestige, and in which losing is the famous part.

If you push a crisis too far the game ends in nuclear war, and it refuses to
show you the war. The screen says you have lost and declines to dramatise it.
That message is the single most quoted thing about the game and it is intact
in this copy.

## Rights

Mindscape published it. Mindscape's assets were dispersed after the company
wound down in the 2000s, and the Macintosh build has not been sold by anyone
since the 1980s.

Chris Crawford has made source and materials for several of his games publicly
available over the years, but this disk is a commercial Mindscape release, not
one of those, and no permission from him is claimed or implied here.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `BalanceOfPowerSanIncCrack` — a Macintosh floppy with its
copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus. The
application is named "Balance of Power"; the boot blocks that say which program
to start hold only fifteen characters, so on this copy the file is named
"Balance of Powe". Nothing else was changed.
