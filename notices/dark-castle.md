# Dark Castle (Silicon Beach Software, 1986) — Macintosh

## What this is

Version 1.2 of the Macintosh Dark Castle, by Mark Stephen Pierce (art and
design) and Jonathan Gay (programming).

## Rights

Silicon Beach Software was acquired by Aldus in 1990, and Aldus by Adobe in
1994, so the original rights most plausibly sit with Adobe, who have never
published it.

Z-Software released Return to Dark Castle in 2008 under licence, and Dark
Castle has been converted to numerous consoles over the years. Those are
separate programs. The 1986 Macintosh original has not been sold by anyone in
nearly forty years.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `mac_DarkCastle_1_2` — a preserved 800K Macintosh disk.
The application and its data were copied onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk; the
disk's own System file was dropped.
