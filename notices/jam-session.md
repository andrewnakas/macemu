# Jam Session (Brøderbund, 1988) — Macintosh

## What this is

Jam Session, by Bogas Productions and published by Brøderbund — a music toy
where the keyboard plays licks in key and in time over a backing track, so
that anyone hammering the keys sounds roughly like a musician.

## Rights

Brøderbund's catalogue passed through The Learning Company and Riverdeep and
is now largely with Houghton Mifflin Harcourt. The company chain exists; the
product does not.

Jam Session has not been sold since the early 1990s and is in no current
product.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `JamSession4amMacCrack` — a Macintosh floppy, copy
protection removed by the preservationist known as 4am.

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus.
