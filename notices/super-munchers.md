# Super Munchers (MECC, 1991) — Macintosh

## What this is

Version 1.0 of the Macintosh Super Munchers, taken from a SoftKey hybrid
Macintosh/PC CD-ROM.

## Rights

MECC was sold to SoftKey in 1995 and the catalogue reached Houghton Mifflin
Harcourt by way of The Learning Company and Mattel. Super Munchers has not been
sold in any form for close to thirty years.

## Why Super Munchers and not Number Munchers

Number Munchers is the better-known title and the one people ask for. A working
Macintosh copy does not appear to survive in public archives:

- `munchers-for-mac-by-mecc` is the one Mac-specific upload, and the image is
  truncated — 2.7 MB of a volume that declares itself 6 MB, with the catalogue
  in the missing part. It cannot be read by anything.
- `Number_Munchers_SoftKey_1990` is a hybrid disc whose Apple_HFS partition
  contains only Acrobat Reader, a CompuServe installer and manuals. The game is
  on the ISO 9660 side, for the PC.

The Apple II and MS-DOS versions are well preserved and playable elsewhere. If a
sound Macintosh image turns up, it will be added.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `super-munchers-cdrom` — the hybrid disc. The Apple_HFS
partition was read out of the Apple Partition Map and the application copied
onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk on its own, leaving behind the bundled AOL
installer and Acrobat Reader that the disc also carried.
