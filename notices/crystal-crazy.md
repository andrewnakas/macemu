# Crystal Crazy (Casady & Greene, 1993) — Macintosh

## What this is

Version 1.0.6 of Crystal Crazy, the sequel to Crystal Quest, written by Patrick
Buckland and published by Casady & Greene.

## Rights

Casady & Greene shut down in 2003; its catalogue was not sold on as a whole and
several titles reverted to their individual authors. Crystal Crazy has not been
sold in over twenty years. Unlike Crystal Quest, it has had no remake on any
platform.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `crystalcrazy` — a preserved 20 MB Macintosh disk. The
application and its Basics, Goodies, Nasties and Objectives folders were copied
onto a System 7.5.3 boot disk. The About file was kept out of the startup folder
so the Finder does not open it over the game.

It runs on a black-and-white Macintosh SE on the game's own advice: started on a
colour screen without its MegaDraw acceleration, Crystal Crazy puts up a dialog
saying it will not manage anything like full speed and would be more fun in
black and white.

One limitation worth recording: machfs does not persist Finder flags on
*folders*, only on files, so the four data folders cannot be marked invisible
the way loose data files are. On this title it does not matter, since the game
takes the whole screen. On a windowed title it would mean a few Finder windows
open behind the game.
