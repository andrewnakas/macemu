# Skyfox (Electronic Arts, 1985) — Macintosh

## What this is

The Macintosh release of Skyfox, Ray Tobey's flight-combat game, ported by
Dynamix and published by Electronic Arts. The title screen credits both.

## Rights

Electronic Arts published it and still exists. As with Archon, that is said
plainly rather than glossed: Skyfox is not an orphan work.

The argument for it being here is about this artefact, not the name. The
Macintosh Skyfox is a 1985/86 black-and-white build that EA has never
re-released in any form. There is no compilation, no storefront and no
subscription that contains it. A person who wanted to buy this program could
not, at any price, from anyone.

Skyfox II: The Cygnus Conflict is a different game on different hardware and
does not contain this one.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `SkyfoxSanIncCrack` — a 400K Macintosh floppy with its
copy protection removed by the preservationist working as "san inc".

The disk keeps its own System and boots on an emulated Macintosh Plus. The
System 7.5.3 disk used for most of this catalogue is no good here: the
application is from 1985 and resets the machine under System 7.
