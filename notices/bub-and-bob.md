# Bub And Bob (Macintosh shareware, 1997)

## What this is

Bub And Bob 1.7.3 — a Macintosh arcade game, evidently inspired by Taito's
Bubble Bobble, which opens on a chooser offering English, Swedish, German,
Italian, Spanish and French.

## Rights

Worth naming plainly: **Bubble Bobble is Taito's**, it is actively licensed,
and re-releases are sold today. This is not that game and is not presented as
it — it is an independently written Macintosh shareware title whose name and
premise are clearly borrowed.

That borrowing is the author's, not this site's. If Taito or a licensee
considers the name or the characters infringing, that is a straightforward
reason to remove it and it will be removed on request without argument.

As software, it is shareware, unregistered and unmodified, and has not been
distributed by anyone commercially.

## Provenance

Internet Archive item `tucows_205607_Bub_And_Bob`, from the Tucows Macintosh
archive. The `.sit` expands to a plain folder, copied onto a title disk that
mounts beside the shared Mac OS 8.6 image.
